# LinkedIn Intelligence Chrome Extension Privacy Notice
_Last Updated: November 21, 2025_

This Privacy Notice describes how the **DreamCRM LinkedIn Intelligence Chrome Extension** (“the Extension”, “we”, “us”) collects, uses, and protects data during use. It applies **only** to the Chrome Extension and supplements the main DreamCRM Privacy Policy.

Full DreamCRM Privacy Policy:  
https://dreamcrm.com/privacy

---

## 1. Overview

The LinkedIn Intelligence Chrome Extension enhances your LinkedIn messaging experience by extracting conversation data from LinkedIn and displaying AI-powered insights.  
It may also sync data to your DreamCRM account if you choose to connect it.

The Extension:

- Does not read emails, passwords, or data outside **linkedin.com**
- Does not collect any information unless you explicitly interact with it (e.g., clicking **Sync**)
- Does not sell your data
- Does not access your clipboard, contacts, or other browser activity

---

## 2. Data the Extension Can Access

To function correctly, the Extension only accesses content visible on your LinkedIn messaging tab.

### 2.1 When you are not logged into DreamCRM

If you use the Extension without connecting a DreamCRM account, all processing happens **locally** in your browser.

The extension may read:

- Your LinkedIn conversation messages  
- Profile name and headline of the person you’re chatting with  
- Message timestamps  
- Conversation URLs  

**None** of this data is transmitted outside your device unless you explicitly click a Sync or Send button.

---

### 2.2 When you connect the extension to DreamCRM

If you authenticate via DreamCRM to sync conversations, the extension may collect:

- Sender/receiver names  
- Profile headline and role  
- Message content  
- Message timestamps  
- LinkedIn conversation URL  
- User ID from your DreamCRM tenant  

This data is used strictly to power:

- Conversation summaries  
- AI-drafted replies  
- Relationship Intelligence insights  
- CRM synchronization  

---

## 3. How Data Is Transmitted

Data is transmitted **only** when you explicitly initiate one of these actions:

- Clicking **Sync Conversation**
- Enabling **Continuous Sync** or **Multi-Sync Mode**
- Requesting an AI-generated reply or summary

All data transfers use secure HTTPS to communicate with DreamCRM backend servers.

Background scripts operate **solely** to process sync actions.  
There is **no passive or automatic** hidden data collection.

---

## 4. How We Use the Data

### If using the Extension alone (no DreamCRM login):

- Data is processed locally to display AI suggestions and UI elements.
- Nothing is transmitted or stored outside your browser.

### If connected to DreamCRM:

Your synced data may be used for:

- AI summaries  
- AI-drafted replies  
- Relationship Intelligence metrics  
- CRM workspace sync  
- Activity tracking in your DreamCRM dashboard  

All usage follows the main DreamCRM Privacy Policy.

---

## 5. What We Do Not Collect

The Extension **does not** collect:

- LinkedIn passwords  
- Browsing history outside linkedin.com  
- Credit card or payment information  
- Files or attachments  
- Data from non-LinkedIn websites  
- Cookies from other services  
- Clipboard or keystrokes  

The Extension does **not** scrape any LinkedIn pages outside the messaging experience.

---

## 6. Data Retention

### Without DreamCRM login
No data leaves your browser.  
Nothing is retained.

### With DreamCRM login
Your synced conversations follow DreamCRM’s data retention policy:  
https://dreamcrm.com/privacy

You may delete synced data from your DreamCRM dashboard at any time.

---

## 7. Permissions Explanation

The Extension uses **minimal permissions**:

### activeTab
Used to read content on LinkedIn messaging pages only when active.

### storage
Stores DreamCRM auth tokens and sync preferences using Chrome storage.

### Permissions the extension does *not* request
Unless ever required and explicitly disclosed, the Extension does **not** request:

- `tabs`  
- `history`  
- `identity`  
- `cookies`  
- `webRequest`  

---

## 8. Third-Party Sharing

We do **not** sell or share your data with any third parties.

Data is transmitted only to:

- DreamCRM backend services  
- Ai20Labs LLC infrastructure  

We **never** transmit data to LinkedIn, Google, Facebook, or any analytics providers from the Extension.
